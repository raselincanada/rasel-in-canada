My story

From Bangladesh to building a life in Canada.

This site is about the real journey — the exciting moments, difficult transitions, places I discover, and lessons I learn along the way.

I’m Rasel, the creator behind Rasel in Canada. My goal is simple: document life honestly and create useful content for people who are curious about Canada.

I share travel experiences, everyday life in Halifax, South Asian food and culture, career lessons, newcomer information, and the little details that are hard to understand until you actually live here.

This is not meant to be a perfect picture of Canada. It is one person’s real experience — and that is exactly what makes it useful.
